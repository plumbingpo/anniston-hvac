Keeping Your Montgomery HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
