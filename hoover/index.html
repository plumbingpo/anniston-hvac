How Hoover Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
